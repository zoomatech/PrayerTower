Each file's title page was checked against the book named here. The archive.org files are OCR (`_djvu.txt`)
and keep the scan's page headers, footnotes and OCR errors; Gutenberg files are proofread and keep the
Project Gutenberg header and licence.

## Not found before 1931

- L. W. King's 1910 Hammurabi translation: no plain-text copy on Gutenberg or archive.org (Gutenberg #17150 is C. H. W. Johns's 1903 translation instead); Harper 1904 is used.
- A full English Pyramid Texts or Coffin Texts translation: none before 1931 (Mercer 1952, Faulkner 1969; Coffin Texts later still). Allen 1916 is the partial stand-in.
- Code of Ur-Nammu (first published 1952), Lipit-Ishtar (1948), Laws of Eshnunna (1948), Instructions of Shuruppak (1960s), Enheduanna's hymns (1968): no pre-1931 English translation.
- Langdon's Ur lamentation (CBS 7080) and Kes liturgy are early, partial readings and may not match the modern composite Lament for Ur and Kesh Temple Hymn.
- L. W. King's The Seven Tablets of Creation (1902) is not on Project Gutenberg; Budge's 1921 Enuma Elish translation is used.
- Budge's full Papyrus of Ani translation (1895) is not on Project Gutenberg; Gutenberg #7145 is his short 1920 introduction with extracts.
