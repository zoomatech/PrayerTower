# Gifts, not ads: why Prayer Tower runs on donations

Draft copy, 2026-10-28. The website's promise page (`/promise`, and `/open-circle/promise` in Open Circle's wording) shows this text when Admin → Notebook ideas → "Public ad-free promise page" is on. Edit it here and in `apps/server/src/modules/site/promise.ts`.

## Prayer Tower

**How we pay for it: gifts, not ads**

Prayer Tower runs on gifts from the people who use it and believe in it. Here is why.

- **Ads would make you the product.** An app paid for by ads earns more the longer it holds your attention, so it is built to keep you scrolling and stirred up. We want you to pray, then get on with your day.
- **Ads would decide what you see.** Advertisers pay for attention, so ad-paid feeds push whatever keeps people hooked. On Prayer Tower, a request reaches people because others prayed, not because someone paid.
- **Ads would need your secrets.** Targeted ads run on profiles built from what you share. Prayer requests are some of the most private things people ever write. They are not for sale.
- **Gifts keep us answerable to you.** When the people who use Prayer Tower pay for it, we answer to them, not to advertisers.
- **Every gift is shared.** Gifts pay for the servers and the prayer radio for everyone. Everyone gets at least one radio prayer, and the rest goes out by need, as the first believers shared "with anyone who had need" (Acts 2:45). Givers can choose a person, a group, or everyone.
- **Giving is never required.** Nobody has to give to use Prayer Tower, and giving never buys a better place in the feed.
- **More of your gift does its work.** We use the lowest-fee ways to give we can find, and we publish what comes in and what goes out.
