## Reviewer notes (remove before publishing)

**Requirements this draft is built to meet**
1. **Google Play UGC policy.** Users must accept the terms "before users can create or upload UGC". The terms must define objectionable content, and the app must offer in-app reporting and blocking, ongoing moderation, and "safeguards to prevent in-app monetization from encouraging objectionable user behavior". ([Play UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937)) Sections 5–7 cover this, and token clawback for abuse is the monetization safeguard.
2. **Apple 1.2.** Apps with user content need filtering, reporting with timely responses, blocking, and "published contact information". ([App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)) Sections 6 and 17 cover this.
3. **Apple's minimum EULA terms.** These apply if this document is used as a custom EULA in App Store Connect. They cover acknowledgement, scope, maintenance, warranty, product claims, IP, legal compliance, developer contact, third-party terms and Apple as third-party beneficiary. ([Apple minimum terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/minterms/)) Section 16 covers this.
4. **Apple 3.1.1 and 3.2.2(iv).** Any later sale of tokens inside the app must use In-App Purchase. Collecting money for charities inside the app is allowed only for approved nonprofits that support Apple Pay, so a Common Prayer Fund would need approved-nonprofit status or collection outside the app. ([App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)) Purchases are switched off in the app.
5. **Apple 1.1.5.** Apple rejects "inflammatory religious commentary or inaccurate or misleading quotations of religious texts". The scripture leaves are exact KJV text, and the Community Rules forbid praying for harm.

**Questions for counsel**
- Is 18+ right for the pilot, or 13+ with protections for teens? Apple now rates apps with social feeds and messaging in its 13+, 16+ and 18+ tiers. ([Apple age ratings](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/age-ratings))
- Should disputes go to arbitration with a class waiver, given the pilot's size?
- Is the radio-broadcast license in section 4 enough, given FCC Part 97?
- Should a non-profit or ministry entity operate the app for liability and donations?
