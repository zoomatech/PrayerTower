# More notebook ideas

Found by cross-checking the Development Plan and the Project Management Document against the
ideas inventory. Each is off by default; an admin turns it on under Admin → Notebook ideas
(`notebook_settings`). All of them work in Open Circle.

| Feature | Switch | What members see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glowing urgency light | `urgency_glow_enabled` | The light on an urgent (2) or very urgent (3) request glows and pulses, faster when very urgent. It stays still when the device asks for reduced motion. |
| Quick reactions | `reaction_tray_enabled` | Press and hold a post (or use the "Quick reactions" accessibility action) for "Light a candle" 🕯️ and "Standing with you" ❤️. One of each per member per post; the counts show on the card. Stored in `quick_reactions`, included in "Download my data", and removed with the account. |
| Town on profiles | `member_town_enabled` | Members can add a town on Me. Cards read "Carol in Albuquerque", and an ask-to-pray notice reads "Carol in Albuquerque would like you to pray for this", but only for members of the post's community, and never on an anonymous post. The town is cleared when an account is deleted. |
| Public waitlist | `waitlist_enabled` | `/waitlist` (and `/open-circle/waitlist`) takes an email, an optional name and a note; joining twice changes nothing. Admin → Waitlist makes a one-use, 30-day invite code for the person's edition, to send from Invite codes. Joining is limited to 10 a minute per address. |
| Pray across lines | `cross_community_enabled` | Members of a faith community who opt in on Me see one prayer request a day from another faith community, from someone who also opted in, and can pray for it. Never sensitive or crisis requests, never anyone blocked, and never Open Circle or any neutral-wording community, in either direction. Does nothing while Prayer Tower is the only faith community. |
| Post anatomy lines | `post_lines_enabled` | The post type ("Prayer Request", in script) sits above a line that extends from the poster's name, and a line runs down the left of everything that belongs to the post, as drawn in Mockups 1 and 2 ("identity line extender" and "content line extender"). Nothing else on the card changes. |
| Tier upkeep | `tier_upkeep_enabled`, `tier_upkeep_days` (30) | Notes Page 3: "statuses must be maintained by using credits". A member above the first tier who has not given or spent a token of their own (a gift, reach, a donation, a radio prayer or a pool) within the set days shows, and is resupplied at, one tier lower until they do. Me says how to keep or get back the tier. Prayers they earned are never taken away. |
| Check-in affirmation | `checkin_affirmation_enabled` | Concept document, check-in step: My Promises shows "By checking in, you are affirming before God that you have truly prayed for this person. We take you at your word." Open Circle reads "By checking in, you are saying you truly kept this promise." |
| Why gifts, not ads | `why_gifts_enabled` | Steve 2026-10-28. Me → "Why no ads?" and the website's `/why-donations` (`/open-circle/why-donations` in Open Circle's words) show the seven reasons from docs/copy/DONATIONS_NOT_ADS.md (the `WHY_GIFTS` list in `site/promise.ts`; the app loads it from `GET /why-gifts`, so there is one copy). Give opens Me → Give in the app while giving is on (Admin → Giving, docs/GIVING.md), and the website links the Zeffy page when Zeffy is the provider; "See where gifts go" links the Common Prayer Fund report while it is published (Prayer Tower only). |
| Award prize choice | `prize_choice_enabled` | On accepting the annual award, the winner chooses to keep the prize, donate it, or split it. The winner is paid the whole token prize, then the donated share (all, or half rounded down) moves to the infrastructure fund, so the ledger shows both. The choice is made once. |
| Matching pledges on token pools | `pool_matching_enabled` | A pool's donor can promise to match other members' gifts N to 1 (up to 5) until a cap. Others in the same community give from Me → Token pools; the match comes from the donor's own tokens and stops at the cap or when the donor runs out. Closing the pool returns what is left to everyone who gave, by share. Source: Ethel p.1 item 6 ("philanthropic first post"). Test: `apps/server/test/pools.test.ts`. |

Sources: Mockups 1 and 2 in the Annotated Read (post lines), Notes Page 3 (tier upkeep), PM doc 3.8 (reactions, urgency light), PM doc 2 (award prize), Dev Plan 3.5 (town), concept document Phases 1 and 4 (waitlist, praying across lines).
Tests: `apps/server/test/notebook-extras.test.ts`, `waitlist.test.ts`, `across.test.ts`.
