# Community Rule draft: sharing faith without pressuring people

> **DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED. Steve decides; counsel reviews before it goes in the Community Rules.**
> Written 2026-10-28 for the notebook item (Page 16): "Think hard about allowing offer [unclear] proselytizing while allowing open discussion."
> V1 assumes a Christian audience with one ecumenical feed (Steve, 2026-10-25), so this rule is mostly about pressure **between Christian traditions**, and about anyone who arrives from outside the faith.

## Proposed rule text (for the Community Rules screen)

```
Share your faith freely. Don't pressure people.

You're welcome to share what God has done for you, quote Scripture, and invite someone to pray, talk or visit your church. Please don't:
- keep pushing after someone says "no thanks", ignores you or blocks you
- tell someone their church, tradition or prayers are false, demonic or worthless
- use a prayer request, or someone's crisis, to argue doctrine or recruit them
- send unasked-for messages to recruit people to your church, group or ministry
- ask for money, sign-ups or contact details as part of sharing faith

Disagreeing kindly about beliefs is fine in comments that invite discussion. Mocking or attacking anyone's beliefs is not.
```

## How it would be enforced (uses what's already built)

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| First unwanted recruiting message | The member reports or blocks. Message requests already limit a stranger to one message until the other person replies. |
| Repeated after "no thanks" or a block | A moderator removes it and sends a notice citing this rule. Appeals work as they do for other rules. |
| Doctrine-shaming on a prayer request | It's removed as a comment that breaks the rule. The requester can hide the commenter. |
| Recruiting for money | Handled under the existing "spam, sell, recruit for money" rule. |
| Hate against a religion or group | Handled under the existing hate rule, and the account can be suspended. |

## Why the line sits here (current store policies)

- **Apple 1.1.1** bans "Defamatory, discriminatory, or mean-spirited content, including references or commentary about religion … particularly if the app is likely to humiliate, intimidate, or harm a targeted individual or group." ([App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/))
- **Apple 1.1.5** bans "Inflammatory religious commentary or inaccurate or misleading quotations of religious texts." ([App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/))
- **Google Play Hate Speech** bans apps that "incite hatred against individuals or groups based on … religion." ([Play: Inappropriate Content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9878810))
- **Google Play UGC** requires terms that define objectionable content and behaviors and prohibit them. ([Play: User Generated Content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937))

Sharing faith is allowed. Attacking or pressuring people is what both stores police.
