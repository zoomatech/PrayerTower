# Testing Prayer Tower

Thank you for helping. This page tells you how to install the test app, what to try, and how to tell us what you find.

## 1. Install the app

**iPhone**

1. Open the email from TestFlight on your iPhone and tap **View in TestFlight**. If you don't have the TestFlight app, the link takes you to the App Store to install it first.
2. In TestFlight, tap **Accept**, then **Install**.
3. Open **Prayer Tower** from your home screen.

Test builds expire after 90 days. TestFlight will offer updates; tap **Update** when it does.


## 2. Sign in

1. Tap **Create account** and use the email address the invitation was sent to.
2. Confirm your email from the message Prayer Tower sends you.
3. Answer the few welcome questions. A short tour shows how things work; you can see it again any time from **Me → How Prayer Tower works**.

## 3. What to try

Please do a little each day rather than everything at once.

**Week 1: the basics**
- Pray for three requests and tap **Praying Now** while you pray.
- Post one request of your own (with your name or anonymously).
- Tap **Commit to Pray** on one request and keep the promise when reminded.

**Week 2: community**
- Send an encouragement to someone you prayed for.
- RSVP to an event, or join a group.
- Mark one of your requests as answered, or post a praise.

**Week 3: tokens and reach**
- Look at your tokens under **Me**. Use some to help a request reach more people, or gift them.
- Try **Pray with someone** if you'd like a prayer partner to pray with you.

**Week 4: anything you like**
- Use the app the way you naturally would, and note what you'd change.

## 4. Report a problem or an idea

- In the app: **Me → Send feedback**. Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Your phone model and app version are added for you.
- Something harmful or unsafe in a request or comment: open the request and tap **Report**. A moderator reviews every report.
- Can't open the app at all: email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

A screenshot helps a lot. On iPhone press the side button and volume-up together.

## 5. Your privacy

- Only the words of a request go out by radio, never your name.
- You can block anyone from their profile.
- **Me → Download my data** gives you a copy of everything on your account.
- **Me → Delete my account** removes your account, posts and activity for good.

The full privacy policy is at [PRIVACY URL].
