## 1. Invitation

**Subject:** You're invited to test Prayer Tower

Dear [FIRST NAME],

I'd like to invite you to be one of [10] people testing **Prayer Tower**, a new app where Christians ask for prayer and pray for one another in real time. When someone taps "Praying Now" on your request, you'll know a real person is praying for you at that moment.

The test runs for **4 weeks, from [START DATE] to [END DATE]**. It takes about 5 minutes a day: pray for a few requests, share your own when you have one, and tell us what works and what doesn't.

**How to join**

1. Watch for a second email from **Apple TestFlight** with the subject "You're invited to test Prayer Tower". It may land in Promotions or Spam.
2. Follow the steps in the tester page: [TESTER INSTRUCTIONS LINK]
3. Sign in with this email address: **[THEIR EMAIL]**

**What to know**

- It's a test, so you may hit a bug. Tap **Me → Send feedback** to tell us; every message is read.
- Prayer Tower never asks for money. Praying earns tokens that help other requests reach more people. Tokens can't be bought or cashed out.
- You can post anonymously, block anyone, and delete your account and everything in it at any time from Me.
- Real moderators read every report. If you or someone you know is in crisis, call or text **[988 / LOCAL CRISIS LINE]**.

Please reply by **[DATE]** to say yes or no either way. Thank you for praying with us.

In Christ,
[YOUR NAME]
