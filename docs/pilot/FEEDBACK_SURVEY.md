# Feedback survey

Paste these into Google Forms or Microsoft Forms. Question types are in brackets. Keep the end-of-pilot survey anonymous unless a tester chooses to leave their email (question 20).

## End-of-pilot survey (about 5 minutes)

**About you**

1. How often did you open Prayer Tower during the test? [Multiple choice: Every day · A few times a week · About once a week · Less than once a week]
2. Which phone did you use? [Multiple choice: iPhone · Android]
3. Which language did you use the app in? [Multiple choice: English · Spanish · Both]

**Praying**

4. "Prayer Tower helped me pray for others more than I usually do." [Scale 1 to 5: Strongly disagree to Strongly agree]
5. When someone tapped Praying Now on your request, how did it feel? [Paragraph]
6. Did you make a Commit to Pray promise? [Yes · No] If yes: did the reminders help you keep it? [Scale 1 to 5]
7. "I trusted that the people praying were real people." [Scale 1 to 5]

**Safety and comfort**

8. "I felt safe sharing a request here." [Scale 1 to 5]
9. Did you see anything that should have been removed? [Yes · No · Not sure] If yes, what? [Paragraph]
10. Did you report anything or block anyone? If so, how did it go? [Paragraph]

**Tokens and reach**

11. "I understood what tokens are for." [Scale 1 to 5]
12. Did you use tokens to help a request reach more people, or gift them? [Multiple choice: Used them for reach · Gifted them · Both · Neither]
13. "Earning tokens for praying felt appropriate." [Scale 1 to 5]

**The app**

14. How easy was Prayer Tower to use? [Scale 1 to 5: Very hard to Very easy]
15. What did you like most? [Paragraph]
16. What frustrated you or got in the way? [Paragraph]
17. Which bug or problem should we fix first? [Paragraph]

**Overall**

18. How likely are you to recommend Prayer Tower to a friend or your church? [Scale 0 to 10]
19. Would you keep using Prayer Tower if it launched publicly? [Multiple choice: Yes · Maybe · No]
20. May we contact you about your answers? If so, leave your email. [Short answer, optional]

## Weekly check-in (3 questions, every Friday)

1. How many days did you open Prayer Tower this week? [Multiple choice: 0 · 1 to 2 · 3 to 5 · 6 to 7]
2. One thing that worked well this week: [Short answer]
3. One thing that didn't: [Short answer]

## Reading the results

Put the averages for questions 4, 7, 8, 11, 13 and 14, and the score for question 18 (the percentage who answered 9 or 10, minus the percentage who answered 0 to 6), into section 4 of [PILOT_RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md](../PILOT_RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md).
